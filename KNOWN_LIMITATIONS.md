# Known limitations

- **Nominal mGEN only.** Curve reserves, initial account balances, creator collateral and protocol subsidies are internal Studionet accounting entries. No native-token backing, real asset transfers, financial protection or adoption is claimed. Each address starts with 300,000 nominal mGEN.
- **Seed data.** The three constructor curves use `origin: "seed_demo"`. Their prefilled verdicts, scores and witnesses are illustrative placeholders, not historical consensus evidence. Seed activity retains this marker after a transaction.
- **Cumulative threshold.** The gate unlocks permanently at 100,000 mGEN of cumulative net buys. Buy fees are excluded; sells do not reduce this total. The current reserve can therefore fall below the threshold while evaluation remains available.
- **Self-reported manifest fields.** `commit_count`, publication fields and delivery summary come from the manifest. Commit history is not independently verified. Claimed tag existence alone is independently checked through GitHub's exact tag-reference API. A tag does not establish code quality, authorship or delivery authenticity.
- **Snapshot and availability.** Both mirrors must identify the same repository path. When both respond, canonical contents must agree. GitHub API errors, rate limits, malformed results or unavailable sources abort the audit before writes. A moved tag or changed manifest can produce disagreement; use immutable commit URLs for repeatable snapshots.
- **Semantic scoring.** After deterministic checks, independently produced LLM scores select BOOST or GRADUATE. Scores describe semantic alignment, not an independent factual attestation. Equivalent results require the same verdict and facts, with score tolerance of 20.
- **Curve shape after boost.** The virtual offset preserves reserve solvency at the slope change. At low supply the reserve integral is clamped at zero. Marginal price is the unadjusted curve derivative; it can differ from an integer trade's actual reserve difference. Holder pool distributions follow current supply, rather than a snapshot of pre-audit holders.
- **Browser session account.** The temporary test account signs real Studionet transactions independently of an injected wallet. Keys exist only in session memory and are discarded on reload or closure. Public pending hashes can resume tracking, but cannot restore signing access to previous positions. There is no key export or recovery feature.
- **Commit-reveal.** Commitments hide rather than encrypt the secret. Use a high-entropy salt and keep a personal copy outside the application. Salt and secret become public transaction inputs at reveal; losing either prevents reveal.
- **Witness scope.** Witnesses preserve contradictory claims and the active verdict. They are attributed allegations, not independently verified corrections or an appeal process.
